The Office 10 desks

Every desk scans every live token once a minute and buys $10 per token, at most 5 open at once, on a $100 paper balance. A token whose liquidity dies is a rug: the position goes to zero. A desk with nothing open says below what it is doing.

Real wallet one desk trades with real SOL

One desk mirrors its paper decisions on Solana the moment it takes them. plumbing mode = every buy capped at $10 while the pipes are checked; full size = the desk's own size. Slippage vs paper is the real fill against the paper price; latency is decision to confirmation. Daily loss cap: past it the wallet stops buying for the day (it still sells). stuck = a sale failed three times and the tokens stay in the wallet. Every signature links to Solscan.

      Score (validation): (min(net %, 300) − max drawdown %) × min(1, trades / 20) when positive − 10 if fewer than 5 trades. The training score is the mean of that over 3 time windows minus half their spread: an agent has to win in every window, not once.

      The desk learns: every day the office looks at each desk's descendants in the hall; if one validates better with no more rugs, the desk adopts its exits and filters (trigger, name, balance and history stay). After 10 trades the office judges the lesson: confirmed, unlearned (only this desk fell), or it was the hour (the whole office fell). Size follows the winner: $5, $10, $20 or $30 per token by equity and trades. ALL-IN = conviction size, several times the usual, when volume and net buyers explode.

      Simulated costs: 1.1 % per side on the bonding curve, 0.5 % once graduated or born straight in a pool; slippage by x·y = k against half the pool's liquidity; $0.30 priority per side. A token that stops having liquidity above $1,000 is a rug and whatever is still inside is worth 0. Signals use only what was known at that minute. Nothing here promises profit: the rugs and the fees eat almost everyone. This is an honest measure.
